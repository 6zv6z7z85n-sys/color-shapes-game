Papitta Play
Multiplayer territory control game. Draw closed figures to capture territory and win!
How to Play
🎯 Objective
Capture territory by drawing closed figures. The player with the most territory wins!
🖱️ Controls
• Click to place points
• Close the shape to capture territory
• Hold to reset zoom
• Scroll to zoom in/out
• 2 Fingers pinch to zoom (mobile)
⚙️ Turns
Players take turns drawing. Each turn you control one shape. After closing it, the next player's turn begins.
📊 Scoring
Your score is the total area of all shapes you've captured. Larger and more shapes = higher score!
